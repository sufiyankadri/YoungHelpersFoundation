

Young Helpers Foundation
FULLY DYNAMIC NGO, ZAKAT & ROZGAAR MANAGEMENT PORTAL
Complete functional, technical, governance and delivery blueprint | Version 1.0 | 20 October 2026
Source basis: Foundation original Zakat & Rozgaar proposal and final 3-page proposal; IndiaZakat public website, How It Works and policy pages. This is a proposed design, not an approved Shariah or legal policy.
1. Executive vision and success criteria
Build a responsive bilingual NGO website and secure operations platform that connects donors, verified beneficiaries, volunteers and the Foundation. Core mission: Aaj Madad, Kal Khud-Mukhtari. It must work for 3–5 initial volunteers, with modular growth later.
One platform: public CMS, causes, donations, Zakat ledger, applicant verification, approvals, disbursement and livelihood outcomes.
Primary outcomes: verified cases supported, transparent fund balances, faster case processing, number of livelihoods started and sustained at 30/90/180 days.
Non-goals for launch: open multi-NGO marketplace, public personal KYC, automatic AI eligibility decisions, loans, complex ERP or mobile apps.

6. Donor journey and donations
Explore verified cause → select Zakat/Sadaqah/General fund and donation amount → donor details and consent → payment order → gateway checkout → signed webhook validation → reconciliation → posted ledger → receipt and donor dashboard. Support one-time first; recurring contributions only after operational readiness.
Donation status: Created, Pending, Authorized, Captured, Failed, Refunded, PartiallyRefunded, Reconciled.
Payment gateway fees must be separately recorded. Do not advertise 100% pass-through unless actually funded/verified.
Cause-linked donations must preserve donor restrictions and prevent inappropriate reallocations.
Manual UPI/NEFT: upload reference, finance verification, bank reconciliation; never treat screenshot alone as confirmed payment.
Receipts: unique number, amount, date, payment method, fund type, applicable tax disclosures; 80G only if valid approval and required data exist.
Refunds and chargebacks reverse the financial effect with audit trail; never delete posted entries.
7. Applicant intake, verification and approval
Application wizard: (1) applicant/contact; (2) household and dependents; (3) income/assets/liabilities; (4) program and need; (5) quotations/medical papers/skill evidence; (6) declaration, consent and submit. Volunteer-assisted offline intake is supported.
Lifecycle: Draft → Submitted → DocumentReview → MoreInfoRequired → VerificationAssigned → FieldVerified → ShariahReview → CommitteeReview → Approved / Rejected / Deferred → Funding → DisbursementApproved → Delivered → FollowUp → Closed. Maintain reason codes and status history. Urgent medical fast-track can shorten queues, not bypass core eligibility controls.
Duplicate indicators: phone, normalized name, household reference and document fingerprint; human review before merging.
Verification checklist: identity, family income, dependents, other assets, quotations, references, urgency and program-specific need.
KYC and medical documents private; only masked, consented cause narratives published. Avoid collecting full Aadhaar numbers unless lawfully necessary.
Committee: quorum, conflict-of-interest declaration, comments, approval ceiling, recorded decision and re-review appeal.
Public cause publication is separate from case approval; not every beneficiary should be publicly listed.
8. Program-specific workflows
Rozgaar and Skill Se Rozgaar
Capture existing skill, livelihood plan, local demand, requested tools, 1–2 quotations where practical, approved amount, vendor, delivery evidence, asset ownership transfer and starting income baseline. Track first customer/market connection, equipment use, 30/90/180-day earning and obstacles. Asset grants, not loans, unless written policy permits otherwise.
Medical
Record treatment category, hospital/doctor estimate, urgency, government scheme/discount exploration, documents, eligible balance, authorization, permitted payee, invoices and follow-up. Direct hospital/vendor payment is not assumed Shariah-compliant until reviewed.
Emergency
Minimal urgent form, proof of crisis, expedited verifier and approval, eligible support type, delivery acknowledgment and short follow-up. Keep Zakat/non-Zakat eligibility and use separate.
Adopt a Rozgaar
Donor may support a verified livelihood cause or pooled fund. Show anonymized milestone updates and outcome, not beneficiary contact information. Earmarking must be honored; excess or shortfall treatment requires policy and donor consent where relevant.

10. Finance, allocation, disbursement and audit
Use a double-entry-style immutable ledger with balanced debit/credit entries per transaction; separate fund dimension for Zakat, Sadaqah, General and other approved categories. Track restricted allocations by donation/cause, committed vs available balances, disbursement requests, approval, payee verification, payment evidence and reconciliation.
Fund balance = posted inflows − posted outflows; available balance further subtracts valid commitments.
Bank reconciliation imports statement references and matches payment gateway settlements and manual transfers.
No direct database updates to posted transactions; reversals and adjustments require approval.
Reports: collection by fund, disbursement by category, outstanding approvals, donor receipts, gateway fees, unmatched payments, opening/closing balances.
Budget example (not target): 100 donors × ₹10,000 = ₹10 lakh; illustrative allocation 40% Rozgaar, 25% Medical, 15% Emergency, 10% approved education/skills, 10% other eligible needs. All amounts policy-dependent.

Common fields: Id BIGINT/UNIQUEIDENTIFIER, CreatedAtUtc, CreatedBy, UpdatedAtUtc, RowVersion, IsActive where appropriate. Money DECIMAL(18,2), UTC timestamps, unique indexes for receipts/payment IDs, foreign keys, CHECK constraints and status enums. Encrypt sensitive data at rest and in transit; keep document files in private object storage, store only metadata/keys in SQL.
Key relationship rules
One applicant → many applications; one application → zero/one public cause and many verifications/decisions.
One donation → one fund, optionally one cause; one gateway payment → one or more verified events, processed idempotently.
One fund → many ledger transactions; each posted ledger transaction → balanced ledger lines.
One approved application → multiple disbursements (partial releases); total releases cannot exceed approved and available restricted amounts.
One livelihood plan → many follow-ups; one published cause → multiple anonymized updates.

13. Angular / .NET solution architecture
Angular 22: standalone components, lazy-loaded public/admin/donor/applicant routes, typed reactive forms, route guards, interceptors, signals for local state, shared design tokens, i18n, SCSS and responsive tables. .NET 10: modular clean architecture (API, Application, Domain, Infrastructure), EF Core or Dapper with migrations, domain services, validation, background outbox worker and integration tests. SQL Server primary relational store; private file storage; transactional email/SMS provider.
Folder map: frontend/src/app/{core,shared,public,donor,applicant,admin}; backend/{YHF.Api,YHF.Application,YHF.Domain,YHF.Infrastructure,YHF.Tests}; database/{migrations,seed}; docs/{requirements,api,security,operations}. Environments: local, staging, production with secrets outside Git.
14. Security, privacy, Shariah and legal release gates
HTTPS, secure cookie/token handling, MFA for privileged roles, password hashing, account lockout/rate limits, CSRF protection where applicable, CSP and secure file upload scanning.
Least privilege, database encryption/backup, recovery drills, log redaction, data retention, consent, grievance channel and breach response process.
Payment gateway merchant onboarding/KYC and permitted donation categories; no card data stored by YHF.
Verify Society registration, PAN, bank account, 12AB/80G applicability and actual approvals before showing claims or issuing tax-benefit receipts.
Do not accept foreign contributions without legal/FCRA review; gateway should block unsupported foreign sources.
Qualified Mufti to approve mustahiq criteria, ownership/tamlik, medical/vendor payments, permissible training/admin expenses, allocation timing, loans vs grants and fund restrictions in writing.
Obtain consent for photos/stories, minimize ID collection, mask personal data, provide applicant correction and complaint channels.

17. End-to-end test and UAT checklist
Responsive website on mobile/tablet/desktop, Hindi/English, accessibility, SEO and page speed.
Role isolation: donors cannot see others’ records; volunteers only assigned cases; rejected applications cannot be published.
Duplicate submission, upload validation, document permissions, consent withdrawal and retention.
Gateway success/failure/timeout/duplicate webhook/refund/chargeback and bank settlement reconciliation.
Fund restriction checks: Zakat cannot silently fund non-eligible expense; disbursement never exceeds approved/available.
Committee quorum, conflict-of-interest, override, reversal and audit log tests.
Backup restore test, production rollback plan, monitoring alerts and user training sign-off.
18. Operations and launch readiness
Daily: review urgent cases, payment failures, pending verifications and security alerts.
Weekly: committee approvals, bank reconciliation, content review, follow-up queue.
Monthly: fund balance reconciliation, donor aggregate report, beneficiary outcome review, backup restore spot-check.
Define accountable owners: Program Lead, Verification Lead, Finance Officer, CMS Admin, Shariah Adviser; same person may hold operational roles but approval separation must remain.
Document SOPs: donation correction, emergency case, rejected case appeal, incident response, refund, beneficiary complaint and data removal request.
