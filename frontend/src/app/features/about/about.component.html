About Young Helpers Foundation

Bridging the gap between poverty and dignified self-reliance through centralized Zakat and targeted sustainable micro-enterprises.

"Helping Today for a Better Tomorrow"

Young Helpers Foundation was founded on a simple yet revolutionary truth: Relief that only feeds for a week keeps poverty alive for a generation.

By channeling Zakat, Sadaqah, and community contributions into productive livelihood assets, hospital bill settlements, and educational sponsorships, we break the cycle of dependency and transform Zakat receivers into future Zakat givers.

Social Service Immediate medical life-support and disaster crisis rehabilitation.
Economic Khud-Mukhtari Permanent asset grants with 30/90/180-day milestone follow-ups.
Taleem & Moral Guidance Scholarships for bright students and ethical community mentoring.
Governance Oath

Our Zero-Doubt Commitments

  • check_circle
    100% Zakat Pass-Through

    Zero admin fee deductions from the Zakat fund. Operating expenses are supported by separate donor contributions.

  • check_circle
    Strict Tamleek (Ownership)

    Assets are bought and legally transferred to the beneficiary. We do not issue micro-loans or debt.

  • check_circle
    Two-Person Verification

    Every case requires independent physical home checks before any funds are approved or disbursed.
