End-to-End Governance

How Young Helpers Foundation Works

A rigorous, transparent journey from case intake to self-reliant livelihood

01
draw

Case Intake

Beneficiary or local volunteer submits identity, household income, and vendor equipment quotation online or through community desk.

02
fact_check

Field Physical Check

Two assigned field volunteers physically visit the home, check poverty baseline, and verify quotation accuracy with local vendors.

03
gavel

Committee Approval

3-member Shariah and Finance committee verifies Mustahiq eligibility. Case is published anonymously protecting beneficiary dignity.

04
handshake

Tamleek Handover

Funds are disbursed directly to machine vendor. 100% unconditional asset ownership transferred to the family with signed handover.

05
query_stats

30/90/180-Day Audit

Volunteers visit at 30, 90, and 180 days to review customer earnings, solve equipment issues, and confirm sustainable Khud-Mukhtari.

Zero Doubt Philanthropy

Why Thousands Trust Young Helpers Foundation

We combine deep religious adherence with modern enterprise audit trails.

percent

0% Admin Cut on Zakat

Every single rupee donated as Zakat goes 100% to verified Mustahiq assets or hospital bills. Foundation overheads are funded through separate voluntary Lillah.

verified_user

Two-Tier Physical Checks

No desk-only approvals. Every beneficiary home is physically visited by local volunteers to verify genuine destitution before any fund is approved.

account_tree

Permanent Asset Handover

We do not issue micro-debt or small loans. We purchase machinery directly and transfer 100% permanent legal ownership (Tamleek) to the beneficiary.

visibility

Live Double-Entry Ledger

Our public audit ledger tracks every donation inflow and vendor outflow. Donors can view anonymized bank reconciliation receipts in real-time.
