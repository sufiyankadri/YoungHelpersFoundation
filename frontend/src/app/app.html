Ek Zakat → Ek Rozgaar Model

How Young Helpers Foundation bridges the gap between Shariah-compliant Zakat giving and permanent financial independence.

01

Intake & Skill Assessment

We interview the applicant to understand their existing skills (tailoring, motor mechanics, plumbing, culinary) and assess local market demand.

02

Quotations & Verification

Two commercial vendor quotations are procured for the heavy-duty machinery. Volunteer verifies household poverty & Shariah eligibility.

03

Asset Handover (Tamleek)

No cash loans. We purchase the equipment directly and transfer 100% unconditional legal ownership to the beneficiary with signed receipt.

04

30 / 90 / 180-Day Monitoring

Our field mentors visit the site at 30, 90, and 180 days to review customer flow, resolve obstacles, and confirm monthly earnings growth.
